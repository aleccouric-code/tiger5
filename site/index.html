The 19th hole problem

Everybody remembers the bet. Nobody remembers the math.

Nassau on the front, skins on the back, a side pot for fewest putts, somebody covered the cart and somebody else got the beer. By the time you’re at the bar, it’s a mess.

So someone says “we’ll figure it out later.” And later never comes. The guy who won never gets paid, and the guy who lost quietly forgets.

Sandie ends that. Easy to track. Easy to get paid.

How it works

Tee it up. Tap it in. Settle up.

No spreadsheets, no group-chat math, no “I’ll Venmo you later.”

1

Start a round with your group

Pick your course from 230+ in Virginia, Maryland and Myrtle Beach, choose your tees, and add up to three friends. Not listed? Snap the scorecard.

2

Score every hole together

Strokes default to par and putts to two, so a normal hole is one tap. Log beers and rips as you go. Works with weak signal on the course.

3

Settle up at the 19th

Bets and receipts become the fewest possible payments, each with a Venmo button. Everyone sees the same numbers, so nobody can “forget.”

Get paid

The bets settle themselves.

Set up a trip, add side bets like fewest putts or best round, post your own action on the Betting Board, and drop receipts in as you spend. Sandie keeps the running tab and shows who owes who.

  • Bets decided automatically from posted scores
  • Put your own amount on Betting Board picks
  • Receipts split evenly, or between just the people in on it
  • Bets and receipts settle separately, so nothing gets muddled
  • One tap to pay on Venmo. Sandie never touches your money.

Questions

Does Sandie hold or move money?

No. Sandie only keeps track. It works out who owes who and gives you a Venmo button to pay, but the money never goes through the app.

Do I need to download it from the App Store?

No. Open Sandie in your phone’s browser and add it to your home screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the menu, then Install app. It opens full screen like any other app.

What does it cost?

Sandie is free.

Can I score my friends too?

Yes. Add up to three friends when you start a round and enter everyone’s scores from your phone. Their rounds post to their own profile, marked as scored by you.

Is my course in there?

Sandie has 230+ courses across Virginia, Maryland and Myrtle Beach, with more on the way. If yours isn’t listed, choose Other course, enter the rating and slope, and snap the scorecard.

Who can see my scores and my Venmo?

Only your friends. Your Venmo username is visible to friends only, and rounds are shared with friends and the people on your trips.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Scoring keeps working on the course, and your round posts as soon as you’re back in range.
